Where Planning Meets Possibility

North Bengaluru's Foremost Plotted Sanctuary

At Nandi Urban City, thoughtful planning meets modern convenience. Spread across 12 acres, this BIAAPA-approved plotted development is designed to maximize open spaces with 7 beautifully landscaped parks, wide RCC internal roads, and advanced underground infrastructure (UGD & STP).

Located in Devanahalli — the heart of New Bengaluru, the project benefits from rapid growth driven by major KIADB developments spanning over 15,000 acres. With Kempegowda International Airport just 10 minutes away, and seamless connectivity to National Highways and the Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR), residents enjoy unmatched accessibility.

Its well-demarcated villa plots provide a safe, organized, and future-ready living environment surrounded by scenic views of Nandi Hills.
