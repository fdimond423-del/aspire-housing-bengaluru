AP Township – Live Where Comfort Meets Everyday Luxury

Premium 30-acre BMRDA & RERA-approved villa township with 8 themed parks, grand clubhouse, swimming pool, and modern infrastructure strategically located near Electronic City for high growth and long-term value.

Explore 50+ Amenities
Modern Living in a Lush Green Township

Crafted For Families, Designed for Generations

At AP Township, exceptional planning blends seamlessly with modern living. Spread across 30 acres, this BMRDA & RERA-approved premium villa township is crafted to offer a refined lifestyle with wide 30 & 40 ft concrete roads, underground utilities, and thoughtfully curated theme parks.

Designed with over 18,000 trees and 48% open green spaces, AP Township prioritizes health, nature, and recreation. Residents enjoy access to multiple theme parks, sports facilities, jogging tracks, kids’ play areas, senior zones, an amphitheatre, and a fully equipped clubhouse — all planned to elevate everyday living.

The development includes 500 well-demarcated villa plots, ensuring a safe, organized, and future-ready community for families and investors alike.
