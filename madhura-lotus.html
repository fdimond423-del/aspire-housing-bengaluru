Serene Plotted Living

A Boutique Plotted Haven with Seamless Urban Reach

Unlike overcrowded mega-layouts, Madhura Lotus is planned as an intimate, low-density community of just 90 residential villa sites. Designed for families and discerning investors seeking a pollution-free atmosphere surrounded by lush green coconut groves and fresh air, while maintaining instant connectivity to South Bengaluru's commercial centers.

Every plot is demarcated with clear boundary stones, equipped with individual water connection lines, and supported by wide asphalted internal avenues. With full BMRDA sanction and clear titles, buyers enjoy complete safety, seamless bank loan eligibility, and instant registration.
