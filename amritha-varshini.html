The Value of Location, Space, and Freedom to Build

Envisioned as one of South Bengaluru’s landmark plotted communities, Amritha Varshini offers a curated choice of residential villa plots, independent luxury villas, and boutique apartments. Spread across 23 pristine acres, the development seamlessly blends urban connectivity with natural serenity.

Strategically positioned in Guddahatti, Attibele, the project is directly connected by an existing 100-foot wide main road leading to the 970-acre KHB Suryanagar Phase-III layout. Being just 1.6 km from the Bangalore–Hosur Express Highway, residents enjoy frictionless access to Electronic City, Bommasandra, and Koramangala.

With BMRDA approval under APA/LAO No. 142/2012-13, individual electronic e-Khata, and ready registration, Amritha Varshini provides the ideal foundation for immediate home construction or high-yield capital appreciation.

Diverse Living Choices

Designed for Every Lifestyle & Family Need

Whether building an independent custom villa or acquiring ready luxury housing, Amritha Varshini offers versatile ownership options.

1. Exclusive Villa Plots

For buyers who envision creating a custom family home from the ground up. Standard demarcated dimensions include 30'x40' (1200 sq.ft), 30'x50' (1500 sq.ft), and 40'x60' (2400 sq.ft), complete with underground utilities, wide road frontage, and individual BMRDA e-Khata.

2. Premium Independent Villas

Aesthetically crafted 2 BHK, 3 BHK & 4 BHK contemporary architectural villas built on 30x40, 30x50, and 40x60 plots. Built-up areas range from 1,500 sq.ft to 2,600 sq.ft with functional double-height layouts, private terrace gardens, and covered car porch.

3. Luxury Apartments

For professionals and modern families seeking refined apartment living within a secure 23-acre gated ecosystem. Offering well-ventilated Studio, 2 & 3 Bedroom residences with shared clubhouse access, lifts, and reserved parking.

4. Commercial High-Street Plots

A limited selection of commercial plots fronting the 100-foot wide main road, ranging from 2,400 to 4,000 sq.ft. Perfectly zoned for supermarkets, clinics, banking branches, and daily convenience shopping serving over 1,500 nearby families.
